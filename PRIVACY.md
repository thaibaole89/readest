# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

My Book Reader is a personal, non-commercial build of the open-source
[Readest](https://github.com/readest/readest) ebook reader, maintained at
<https://github.com/thaibaole89/readest>. It is intended for use by its
maintainer only.

## Google user data

The app requests one Google scope: `drive.file`. This lets it see and manage
only the files and folders it creates in your Google Drive. It cannot read
anything else in your Drive.

- **What it stores:** your book files, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and
  library information, saved in your own Google Drive.
- **Why:** to keep your library and reading position in sync between your
  devices.
- **Where sign-in tokens live:** on your device, in its secure storage.
- **Sharing:** Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and
  not sent to any server run by the maintainer. There is no such server.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other data

Usage analytics and crash reporting are switched off in this build, and it
does not use Readest Cloud.

Optional features you choose to use, such as translation, dictionary lookup or
read-aloud, may send the selected text to the third-party service that provides
them.

## Removing your data

Disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings, revoke access at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>, and delete the app's folder from
your Google Drive.
