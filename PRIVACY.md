# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Readi is a free, non-commercial ebook reader built on the open-source
[Readest](https://github.com/readest/readest) project. Its source code is at
<https://github.com/thaibaole89/readest>. There is no Readi account and no
Readi server: your library stays on your device and, if you choose, in your
own cloud storage.

## Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, the app requests one Google scope: `drive.file`.
This lets it see and manage only the files and folders it creates in your
Google Drive. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.

- **What it stores:** your book files, reading progress, bookmarks, notes and
  library information, saved in your own Google Drive.
- **Why:** to keep your library and reading position in sync between your
  devices.
- **Where sign-in tokens live:** on your device, in its secure storage.
- **Sharing:** Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and
  not sent to any server run by the developer. There is no such server.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## AI features

AI features (book summaries, library tidy-up, book recommendations) are off
until you turn them on and choose an AI provider: a service you sign up for
with your own API key, such as Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek or
OpenRouter, or a model running on your own computer. Before the first request
to an online provider, the app asks for your consent.

When you use an AI feature, the text it needs (for example a chapter, or book
titles and authors from your library) is sent directly from your device to the
provider you chose, under that provider's own privacy policy. It does not pass
through any Readi server, and your API key stays on your device. A model on
your own computer receives the text locally.

## Other data

Usage analytics and crash reporting are switched off in current builds, and the
app does not use Readest Cloud.

Other optional features you choose to use, such as translation, dictionary
lookup or online read-aloud voices, may send the selected text to the
third-party service that provides them.

## Removing your data

Disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings, revoke access at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>, and delete the app's folder from
your Google Drive. Uninstalling the app removes what it stored on your device.
